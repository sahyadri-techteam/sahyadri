As a seasoned professional in energy efficiency, environment conservation, and climate change, I have dedicated the past 30 years to academic, research, and consulting roles in these areas. My academic background includes Master's degrees in engineering, management, and research from esteemed government institutions in India and abroad.

My recent work has focused on the conservation of heritage seeds and the promotion of Agroecology-based farming practices within our rural neighborhood at Sahyadri School. Through a decade-long effort, I have established school and village-level native seed banks, working in collaboration with a team of like-minded farmers and supported by the school management, conscious parents, and other visitors to the campus.

I find solace in open landscapes and am committed to their upkeep, taking on a caretaking role to ensure their preservation for future generations.
