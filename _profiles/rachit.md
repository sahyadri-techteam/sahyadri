I joined Sahyadri School in Grade Grade 4(2019). One thread runs through what I do: trying to make complex things feel human. In computer science, that's a research project on trust and reputation between AI agents, using poker as a testbed to study cooperation and deception — work I'm now preparing for publication. On stage, I've had lead roles in Vriksha, Anyaay, Rehearsal, and Twelve Angry Jurors, as well as hosting the school farewell. Off stage, I captain the handball team and edit for Tiwai Tales and Ninad '26. Through the outdoor programme, I've trekked to Bali Pass (16,000 ft) and Pandav Patthar (12,500 ft), and beyond the school calendar, I built Lifeline, a refugee-communications app that won the Hilltop Entrepreneur award. I joined Sahyadri in Grade 4 in 2019. Over the eight years, I have grown in ways I could not have imagined. What I have come to value most is the space the school provides for things beyond the classroom — I discovered theatre here, learned to hold a stage, and had the opportunity to trek to the Himalayas. The quieter moments have stayed with me just as much: Astachal at the end of the day, and the space to think rather than just compete. Life here has not always been easy, but it has never felt narrow.
