I joined Sahyadri School in Grade 4(2019).

One thread runs through what I do: trying to make complex things feel human. In CS, that's a research project on trust and reputation between AI agents, using poker as a testbed to study cooperation and deception — work I'm now preparing for publication.

On stage, I've had the opportunity to take on lead roles in Vriksha, Anyaay, Rehearsal, and Twelve Angry Jurors, as well as hosting the school farewell. Off stage, I captain the handball team and edit for Tiwai Tales and Ninad '26. Through the outdoor programme, I've had the chance to trek to Bali Pass (16,000 ft) and Pandav Patthar (12,500 ft), and beyond the school calendar, I built Lifeline, a refugee-communications app that won the Hilltop Entrepreneur award.

I joined Sahyadri in Grade 4, in 2019. Over the eight years, I have grown in ways I couldn't have imagined. What I've come to value most is how much room the school makes for things beyond the classroom — I found theatre here, learned to actually hold a stage, and got to trek to the Himalayas. The quieter parts have stayed with me just as much: Astachal at the end of the day, and the space to think rather than only compete. Life here hasn't always been easy, but it has never felt narrow.

I've cherished the opportunities to explore my passions and interests, and I'm grateful for the support and guidance I've received along the way. Sahyadri has been more than just a school to me – it's a community that has helped shape me into the person I am today.
