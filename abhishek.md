Abhishek takes English in the senior school. With a Master’s degree in English and a Bachelor of Education, he boasts over a decade-long experience in school teaching. Reading Krishnamurti and learning about how Sahyadri organically allows K’s ideas to harmonize with daily school life, including its academic aspect, inspired him to join Sahyadri School. Finding equal fulfillment in both interacting with people and solitude, he cherishes the enriching contrasts they offer. Alongside his wanderlust, which grants him a unique sense of freedom, he immerses himself in a wide array of literary works. Theatre has been a passion for him for over a decade, actively participating in school productions and also contributing to Sahyadri’s vibrant theatre.

He loves Sahyadri’s tryst with different seasons and enjoys being dazzled by its ever-changing landscapes.
