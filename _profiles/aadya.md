I joined Sahyadri School in Grade 7th Grade.

Events: Head of the farewell gifts committee (2025-26), Sailing Assembly (with Kaeya), Swiftonomics Assembly (with Anaya Arora), Helped organize Art and Lit Fest 2025, Joined Phoenix Club in 9th Grade, been running Phoenix club (with Naina) since the beginning of 11th grade, Core member and editor of Newsletter committee, 2025-26 Ninad Editing Team, Land-care committee, student council & dorm visits, Participation in the ESD programme for about three years, (some clubs I want to join and a club I am helping with but not running is in progress), Helping organize the school's first alumni meet.

I joined Sahyadri because I wanted an education that was different from the conventional path. The school's emphasis on independent thinking, community living, and learning beyond the classroom appealed to me.

Life at Sahyadri has been complicated. I have not always found it easy to fit in, and there were many aspects of residential life that challenged me. At the same time, I have never felt entirely at home elsewhere either. In many ways, being at Sahyadri forced me to confront that feeling and learn how to navigate it.

Despite the difficulties, Sahyadri has played a major role in shaping who I am today. It encouraged me to think for myself, pursue my interests seriously, and develop a sense of independence. Many of the projects, ideas, and interests that are important to me now grew out of my time here.

My experience at Sahyadri has not been defined by comfort, but by growth. The school challenged me intellectually and personally, and those challenges have had a lasting influence on how I see the world and my place in it.
