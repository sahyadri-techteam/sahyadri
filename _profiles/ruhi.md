I joined Sahyadri School in Grade 7.

Throughout all the years I've been here, there's always something or other to do. I've participated in all the plays done by my batch, from the SS Fest in 7th grade to the Mughal Fest in 8th grade, the Mahabharat play in 9th grade to the Carpe Diem play in 10th grade.

In addition to these plays, I've also had the opportunity to showcase my talents in other areas. I've given a Bharatanatyam performance and have played interschool tennis twice.

I joined Sahyadri mainly because my parents were associated with the school even before I was born and wanted me to study here. Also, the education from where I'm from did not provide me with the guidance I needed, and my parents thought it would be better if I came here to study. Little did I know that I would learn so much beyond just academics.

I'm grateful for the opportunities I've had here, and I've been fortunate to have been a part of various activities and events. My parents' decision has been a great one, and I'm thankful for their support.
